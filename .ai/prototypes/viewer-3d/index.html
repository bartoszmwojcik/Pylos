Pylos viewer: 3D replay and live screens

Design proposal for S4 (web viewer, Later scope) from .ai/specs/product-brief.md. Direction from the owner: 3D board views in the spirit of hpylos; cold, metallic, sans-serif, slightly squarish. Games are real records from pylos-ref playout. Backend behaviour is simulated in the browser. See README.md.

2. Replay a game (representative screen)

Step through a recorded game and see each move on the 3D board: where the ball went, the shape it completed and which balls came back.

PYLOS
games/2026-11-22-seed-0007.json localhost:8080 · connected
1Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Camera presets: Perspective, Top, Side, Corner. Levels hides upper levels so you can look inside the pyramid.
2The last move has an ice ring. A raise draws a dashed arc from a translucent ghost at its origin. Taken-back balls stay as translucent ghosts for that ply. Balls of a completed shape get a thin ring.
3Arrow keys step through the moves while the board has focus, Home and End jump, and Space plays. Hover a ball to see its square (b22 · White · level b).
4The move text is generated from the record. The engine’s own wording for moves is not specified anywhere; this is a proposal.
